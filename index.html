Pipeline Pillars

Streaming Ingest & Deterministic Audit

An in-RAM ffprobe audit on a 2MB head slice (with MOOV-atom fallback) verifies every source before a byte hits disk. Unsupported inputs — 10-bit, malformed — fail fast with a legible reason, never a retry storm.

Content-Addressed Chunk Ledger

Sources split into 4-second chunks, each fingerprinted with a content ID in the Postgres chronicle — master_cid identifies the whole asset like an infohash. Dispatch is fail-closed — if the chronicle can't verify, nothing runs. Re-runs are differential: only missing or failed chunks ever re-dispatch.

VRAM-Invariant GPU Transcode

One NVDEC decode feeds three scale_cuda rungs into three h264_nvenc encodes — the full ABR ladder per chunk without frames ever touching system RAM. Concurrency is engineered to the measured NVENC session ceiling, not guessed.

Local HLS Origin & Secure Playback

The master playlist is authored only from hash-verified segments, with BANDWIDTH declared from measurement — never from a template. A local origin serves it to a vendored Shaka console behind short-TTL signed JWTs — zero-trust playback with no CDN bill.

It Verifies Itself

The pipeline audits its own output: an 8-tier conformance gate sweeps every run, fixes happen under written charters, and every fix is proven by a control. The defect register is append-only at 84 rows — including one open finding (~21 ms audio surplus at some span boundaries), stated rather than hidden.

Edge Intelligence

Native FFmpeg scene detection maps cuts onto the ledger; keyframes flow through a SigLIP vision model (ONNX, CUDA) into local sqlite-vec. Ask for a moment in plain language and it answers on-machine — no cloud round-trip, no frame export. In-VRAM 9:16 verticalization remains on the roadmap.
